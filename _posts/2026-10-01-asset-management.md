## Headwinds Facing Asset Management in 2026

1. **Fee compression** — Passive index funds (BlackRock, Vanguard) have driven fees to near-zero. Traditional firms are managing record AUM but generating less profit from it.

2. **Tech capex burden** — Surviving in 2026 requires massive investment in cloud infrastructure, tokenization, and advanced AI analytics. Smaller independent firms often lack the cash flow to fund these upgrades.

3. **Private markets shift** — Investors are moving away from public stocks and bonds into private markets (private credit, real estate, infrastructure). Mid-sized managers without specialized teams are forced to acquire alternative specialists or sell themselves to a larger firm.

### Vanguard–Blackstone–Wellington Breakdown

- **Vanguard** — famous for hyper-frugal, low-fee index funds
- **Blackstone** — complex alternative investments with higher fees, typically restricted to institutional/ultra-wealthy investors
- **Wellington** — active management with deep equity and fixed income research
