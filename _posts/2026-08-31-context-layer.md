## What is a Semantic Layer

A semantic layer is an abstraction that sits between your data sources — warehouses, lakes, OLAP engines — and the consumers of that data — dashboards, applications, and now agents. Its job is to translate the technical reality of your tables into the business reality your organization speaks.
The semantic layer is the dictionary that maps the language of the business onto the structure of the data, so that everyone — and every agent — gets the same answer to the same question.
